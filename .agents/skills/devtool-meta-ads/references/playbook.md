# Meta ads playbook

## Fictional product context

TraceNest is a fictional local HTTP replay tool. An engineer can replay a sanitized fixture locally, but service integration needs a platform owner. Sample replay is not proof of recurring project value. Do not invent hosted behavior, speed improvements, or customer results.

## Decision to resolve

Judge whether a verified task demonstration can reach relevant engineers in an interruption channel. Broad interests do not establish a developer role or deployment authority. Connect engagement to useful task evaluation, with a capped hypothesis test.

## Task-specific method

Show a real task or input/output change. Avoid claiming that broad interest targeting identifies engineers with production authority.

## Application brief

Deliver: Channel-fit judgment, creative briefs and copy, offer and landing path, budget assumptions, qualification definitions, and measurement limitations.

Do not assume missing capabilities or report proposed tests as completed. A useful answer identifies what is known, finishes the requested artifact within that scope, and names the next bounded verification when needed.

## Review criteria

Do not recommend retargeting or customer-list uploads without checking lawful and authorized data use. Cheap clicks or downloads alone do not establish channel fit.

The [shared method examples](shared-methods.md) provide additional templates and cross-product examples; apply only those that fit the product scope.
