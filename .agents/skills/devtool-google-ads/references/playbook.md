# Google ads playbook

## Fictional product context

TraceNest is a fictional local HTTP replay tool. An engineer can replay a sanitized fixture locally, but service integration needs a platform owner. Sample replay is not proof of recurring project value. Do not invent hosted behavior, speed improvements, or customer results.

## Decision to resolve

Classify learner, job, support, free-package, commercial-tool, and production-task queries before excluding or bidding. Define qualified own-project evaluation and recurring use; do not optimize a campaign solely for low-cost signups.

## Task-specific method

Check whether searches seek a free package, a tutorial, an existing product login, or a team solution. Optimize toward useful evaluation and recurring project use where measurable.

## Worked application

For this scenario, apply the task-specific method above and deliver: Campaign and ad-group structure, example ads, intent and exclusion rationale, landing-path requirements, budget scenarios, and downstream measurement and decision rules.

Do not assume missing capabilities or report proposed tests as completed. A useful answer identifies what is known, finishes the requested artifact within that scope, and names the next bounded verification when needed.

## Review criteria

Do not default to business-hours delivery, mobile bid cuts, or a fixed conversion-count rule. Do not claim causality from attributed pipeline or upload sensitive customer data without authorization.

The [shared method examples](shared-methods.md) provide additional templates and cross-product examples; apply only those that fit the product scope.
