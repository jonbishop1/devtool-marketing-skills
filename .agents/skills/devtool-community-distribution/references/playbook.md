# Community distribution playbook

## Fictional product context

TraceNest is a fictional local HTTP replay tool. An engineer can replay a sanitized fixture locally, but service integration needs a platform owner. Sample replay is not proof of recurring project value. Do not invent hosted behavior, speed improvements, or customer results.

## Decision to resolve

Choose stack-specific communities, maintainers, or package ecosystems where the task is relevant. Offer a reproducible answer and disclose affiliation. Respect project governance and maintain the example rather than turning issue trackers into promotion channels.

## Task-specific method

Work with stack-specific communities, maintainers, package ecosystems, examples, and integration workflows. Respect open-source project governance and avoid turning issue trackers into ad channels.

## Completed example

Draft reply: "To distinguish a replay-tool problem from a service problem, first confirm that the local service is running, then compare its response to a controlled fixture's expected status/body. Keep credentials out of the fixture. [Affiliation: add your actual relationship to the product before posting.] If useful, I can share the fixture structure." This supplies a useful method without inventing maintainer identity or execution evidence. No post was sent.

Do not assume missing capabilities or report proposed tests as completed. A useful answer identifies what is known, finishes the requested artifact within that scope, and names the next bounded verification when needed.

## Review criteria

Do not post, open promotional issues, contact maintainers, or send messages without authorization. No fake accounts, astroturfing, or unsolicited bulk outreach.

The [shared method examples](shared-methods.md) provide additional templates and cross-product examples; apply only those that fit the product scope.
