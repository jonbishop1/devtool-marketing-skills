# Sales enablement playbook

## Fictional product context

TraceNest is a fictional local HTTP replay tool. An engineer can replay a sanitized fixture locally, but service integration needs a platform owner. Sample replay is not proof of recurring project value. Do not invent hosted behavior, speed improvements, or customer results.

## Decision to resolve

Turn an individual successful task into an honest team-evaluation memo: dependency and maintenance owner, integration effort, deployment and security review, commercial fit, reversibility, and an owned next test. Do not call a local demo production adoption.

## Task-specific method

Connect individual technical success to team maintenance, deployment, procurement, security, and integration ownership. Repository popularity alone does not establish organizational need.

## Worked application

For this scenario, apply the task-specific method above and deliver: A discovery or evaluation plan, role map, evidence-backed objection table, champion brief, and owned next step.

Do not assume missing capabilities or report proposed tests as completed. A useful answer identifies what is known, finishes the requested artifact within that scope, and names the next bounded verification when needed.

## Review criteria

Do not turn a successful demo into a claimed customer outcome. Do not treat unverified certifications or roadmap items as currently available.

The [shared method examples](shared-methods.md) provide additional templates and cross-product examples; apply only those that fit the product scope.
